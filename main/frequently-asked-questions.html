What services do you offer?

At KenyaLink Facilitators, we offer a wide range of migration services including eligibility evaluation, regulatory updates, alternative visa options, document assistance, application submission, and collaboration with immigration authorities.

How long does it take to process a work permit in Kenya?

The processing time for a work permit in Kenya varies depending on the type of permit and the specific circumstances of the applicant. On average, it can take 2 to 6 months for approval by the Directorate of Immigration Services. It is recommended to apply well in advance and ensure that all required documents are submitted correctly to avoid delays.
