Permits:


A Work Permit in Kenya, issued under the Kenya Citizenship and Immigration Act, 2011, enables foreign nationals to engage in various activities such as trade, business, and professional employment.

Class C : Prescribed Profession

A professional license in Kenya is given to someone in a designated field who plans to practice that profession.

Class D : Employment

This permit is for individuals offered specific employment by an employer in Kenya.

Class G : Business or Consultancy

Given to those engaging in specific trade, business, or consultancy.

Class I : Religious or Charitable Activities

For missionaries and individuals engaged in charitable activities under registered institutions.

Class K : Ordinary Residents

For individuals with independent income who wish to reside in Kenya.

Class M : Refugees

Given to individuals with refugee status in Kenya.
