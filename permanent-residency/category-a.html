PERMANENT RESIDENCY : Category A

‍

Before You Apply:

  • Citizenship Status: Applicants were Kenya citizens by birth.
  • Denial of Dual Citizenship: They have renounced or lost Kenyan citizenship status and are denied the opportunity by their countries of domicile to hold dual citizenship.
  • Eligibility for Family: Children and spouses of the mentioned applicants are also eligible to apply.

Disclaimer:

  • An application disclaimer is applicable.

How to Apply:

  1. Submission of an application letter.
  2. Duly filled Permanent Residence Application Form (Form 23), signed, and stamped by a Magistrate/Commissioner for Oaths.
  3. Access and fill Category A - Questionnaire for Intending Applicants during the online application.

Who Qualifies:

  • Applicants who were Kenya citizens by birth but have renounced or lost Kenya citizenship status and are denied the opportunity by their countries of domicile to hold dual citizenship.
  • Children and spouses of the above-mentioned applicants are eligible.

Requirements:

  1. Proof of Previous Kenya Citizenship:
  2. Copy of birth certificate/Identity card or Passport.
  3. Photographs:
  4. Two recent passport size photographs (soft copy required during application).
  5. Police Clearance Certificate:
  6. Issued by the country of domicile.
  7. Proof of Current Citizenship:
  8. Copy of registration/naturalization certificate and passport.
  9. Additional Documents:
  10. Copy of the Police Clearance Certificate.
  11. Copy of the Checklist (downloadable from the provided link).

Ensure all documents are accurately filled, signed, and provided before submission.
