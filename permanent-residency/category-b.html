PERMANENT RESIDENCY : Category B

‍

Before You Apply:

  • Application applies to those with at least seven (7) years of Kenya work permits.
  • Continuous residency for three (3) years preceding the permanent residence application.
  • Spouses and children of the mentioned applicants are included.

Requirements for Applicants:

  • Proof of lawful residence on work permits for a minimum of seven (7) years.
  • Continuous three (3) years residency in Kenya before applying.
  • Strong potential for socio-economic contribution, especially for investors and highly qualified professionals.

Documentation:

  • Application letter
  • Duly filled permanent residence application form (form 23)
  • Category B - Questionnaire for Intending Applicants
  • Residence status form
  • Copy of valid passport
  • 2 passport size photographs
  • Police Clearance Certificate (Original)
  • Copy of valid tax compliance certificate
  • Copy of valid foreigner certificate (Alien card)
  • Introductory letter from employer (for employees)

Requirements for Spouses:

  • Same as above for applicants, plus:
  • Certified copy of the marriage certificate
  • Copy of dependent pass/work permit(s)

Requirements for Children:

  • Same as above for applicants, plus:
  • Certified copy of the birth certificate
  • Certified copies of the parents' birth certificates
  • Copy of dependent pass

Note: All documents must be certified, and a soft copy of photographs is required during the application process.
