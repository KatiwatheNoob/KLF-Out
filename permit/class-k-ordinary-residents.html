CLASS K : Ordinary Residents

Requirements For New Applications:

‍

1. Authorization:

  • Original Authorization Letter for KenyaLink Facilitators Limited (if using their services).

2. Immigration Status:

  • Copy of Immigration Status if currently in Kenya.

3. Proof of Assured Income:

  • Documentary proof of assured annual income (bank statements, proof of assets) from sources outside Kenya.
  • Assured annual income must amount to USD 24,000 Per Annum.
  • Bank statements must be from a local bank account (funds transferred into a local bank account).

4. Application Form:

  • Duly filled and signed application Form 25.
  • Applicant fills the "Personal Particulars" section and Part 3, then signs.

5. Cover Letter:

  • Original cover letter from the applicant:
  • Stating the desire to apply for retirement in Kenya.
  • Confirming investments abroad to support the stay in Kenya.
  • Undertaking NOT to accept paid employment of any kind.
  • Addressed to THE DIRECTOR OF IMMIGRATION SERVICES, Nyayo House, Nairobi, KENYA.
  • Provide Kenyan contact details (phone number, address, and email) on the letterhead.
  • The letter must be signed.

6. Passport Documents:

  • Copies of the National Passport.

7. Passport Photos:

  • Two recent passport-size photographs (not older than 6 months).

8. Fees:

  • Processing Fee: KES 20,000 (bankers cheque to PRINCIPAL SECRETARY MINISTRY OF INTERIOR AND COORDINATION OF NATIONAL GOVERNMENT).
  • Application Fee: KES 500,000 for 2 years (option for 1 year at KES 250,000, bankers cheque to PRINCIPAL SECRETARY MINISTRY OF INTERIOR AND COORDINATION OF NATIONAL GOVERNMENT).
  • Payment after permit approval with a grace period of a month.

9. Security Bond:

  • Submit the Security Bond form to Immigration along with the application fee.

10. Processing and Endorsement:

  • Once payments are submitted, the final permit will be processed after a few days and endorsed in the applicant’s passport.
