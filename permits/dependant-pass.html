Dependent Pass


Who qualifies?:


a) Kenya citizens.

b) permit holders.

c) Exempted persons.

d) Permanent residents.


Requirements

  • Detailed and Signed Cover letter from the applicant addressed to the Director of Immigration Services
  • Form 28 duly filled and signed.
  • Copy of a valid national passport / National ID (bio-data page) for both the applicant and the Dependant.
  • Two recent passport-size photos of the dependent.
  • Evidence of the relationship between the applicant and the dependant i.e. duly certified copies of marriage certificate, birth certificate, and death certificate, whichever applicable
  • Proof of sufficient and assured income to sustain self and the dependant.
  • Documents in foreign languages should be translated into English by either the Embassy, Public Notary, or authorized /recognized institution
  • Documentary Proof of any other condition leading to dependency e.g. Medical condition, disability, age among others.
  • A valid copy of permit for those applicants holding a work permit (at least six months prior to date of application).

Fees:

    Issuance fee (payable upon approval)

    • Ksh 20,000 for spouse and children of Kenyan Citizen.
    • Ksh 10,000 for Dependant Pass for work permit and Permanent Residency holders

    Processing Fees - Nil

Note!

  • All payments must be made through the provided online module.
  • It is an **offense** to mislead an Immigration Officer (Kenya Citizenship & Immigration Act, 2011 - Section 53 (1)(a)).
  • Failure to make payment within 30 days from approval will render it null and void (Section 56 of the Kenya Citizenship & Immigration Regulations, 2012).
  • A Dependent Pass does not allow the holder to study without a valid Pupil's Pass.
  • Notification of approval is not an authority to engage in business, profession, employment or residence without first obtaining the dependant Pass.
  • Additional documents may be required upon request.
  • East African Community citizens (Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Burundi) are exempted** from Dependent Pass fees.
  • A Dependent Pass is not issued to persons already engaged in employment.
  • It is an offence while in possession of a dependant pass to engage in any form of trade or employment either for gain or not.
  • It is not intended for visa fee evasion.
  • Accepted Payment Methods: M-PESA, Airtel Money or Bank Transfer.
