Re-Entry Pass:

‍

1. Duration:

  • 2-year re-entry pass for most cases.
  • 1-year re-entry pass for those with spouses holding a 1-year permit.

2. Renewal:

  • Renewed every time the spouse’s entry permit is renewed.
  • Dependent Passes do not require renewal.

3. Expiration:

  • Dependent Passes automatically expire when the spouse’s entry permit expires.

Requirements:

‍

1. Form:

  • Fill the Re-Entry pass form.

2. Documents for Dependent Pass Holder:

  • Copy of the Dependent Pass.
  • Copy of the passport endorsement on the Dependent Pass.
  • Copy of the passport (bio-page) of the Dependent Pass holder.

3. Documents for Spouse (Permit Holder):

  • Copy of the Spouse’s permit.
  • Copy of the passport endorsement on the Spouse’s Permit.
  • Copy of the passport of the Permit Holder.

4. Original Passport:

  • Original passport of the Dependent Pass Holder for the purpose of endorsing the Re-entry Pass.

5. Cash Fees:

  • KES 2,000 cash for a 2-year Re-entry Pass.
  • KES 1,000 cash for a 1-year Re-entry Pass.
