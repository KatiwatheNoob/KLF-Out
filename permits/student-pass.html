Student Pass Requirements

1. Application Form
  • A duly filled, signed and stamped application Form 30 by the institution's authorized personnel (both new and renewal cases)
  • You can print the form at the end of the application or reprint it from the "My Applications" section
2. Cover Letter
  • Detailed and signed cover letter addressed to the Director of Immigration Services from the institution
  • Must indicate the course to be pursued and the duration (both new and renewal cases)
3. Financial Documentation
  • Signed commitment letter from the sponsor (both new and renewal cases)
  • National passport copy of the sponsor (both new and renewal cases)
  • Proof of funds for self-sponsored students (both new and renewal cases)
4. Passport and Immigration Documents
  • Copy of a valid national passport - bio-data page (both new and renewal cases)
  • Current immigration status documentation (if already in the country)
  • Copy of student's pass previously held (for renewal cases only)
5. Photographs
  • Two recent passport-size color photos (both new and renewal cases)
6. Academic Documentation
  • Certified copies of basic academic certificates for those joining Tertiary Institutions and Universities (new cases)
  • Certified copies of progress report/transcripts obtained (for renewal cases)
7. For Minors
  • Consent letter from the parent (both new and renewal cases)
  • Copy of parent's national passport
  • Copy of birth certificate of the minor as proof of relationship
  • For non-boarders, attach a copy of parent's Kenya residence permit
8. Institution Documentation
  • Copy of the school's/college registration certificate from the Ministry of Education (new cases)
9. Additional Requirements
  • Documents in foreign languages must be translated into English by an Embassy, Public Notary, or authorized institution
  • Police Clearance Certificate from country of origin or Kenya Police Service (if resident)
  • Clearance letter from Department of Refugee Affairs for refugees (both new and renewal cases)
  • Copy of Checklist

Internship/Research Pass Requirements

1. Application Form
  • A duly filled, signed and stamped application Form 30 by the institution's authorized personnel (both new and renewal cases)
2. Cover Letter
  • Detailed and signed cover letter from the institution indicating the internship or research topic to be pursued and the duration
  • Must be addressed to the Director of Immigration Services
3. Passport and Immigration Documents
  • Copy of a valid national passport (bio-data page)
  • Current immigration status documentation (if in the country)
4. Photographs
  • Two recent passport-size color photos
5. Academic Documentation
  • Duly certified copies of academic certificates and CV
6. Research Documentation
  • Copy of research permit from the National Council for Science and Technology (for research programs)
7. Additional Requirements
  • Documents in foreign languages must be translated into English by an Embassy, Public Notary, or authorized institution
  • Police Clearance Certificate from country of origin or Kenya Police Service (if resident)
  • For minors, attach a copy of parent's Kenya residence permit if not a boarder
Note: Please ensure all documents are complete and properly certified where required. Incomplete applications may result in processing delays.

Issuance Fees:

  • Research: Usd 100 per Year
  • Student Pass: Usd 100 per Year
  • Internship: Usd 100 per Year

Mode of Payment

  • Mpesa
  • Airtel Money
  • Bank Transfer

Note

  • Receiving education/training without a Pupil’s Pass is an offence (Kenya Citizenship & Immigration Regulations, 2012 - Section 30(7)).
  • A Student Pass is not an authorization to engage in any employment.
  • Additional documents or documents may be required.
  • EAC citizens (Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Burundi) are exempted from Student/Research/Internship pass fees.
  • Applications should be submitted at least 3 months before studies begin.
